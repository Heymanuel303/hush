! Title: hush — strict mode
! Description: Hard-block whole pages (not just hide) for the phone: Shorts, Reels, TikTok, Explore/Discover feeds, autoplay video. uBO shows a "blocked" interstitial instead of the page.
! Expires: 1 day
!
! uBlock Origin filter list — NOT part of the extension (lives outside src/).
! These use the $document modifier: uBO refuses to load the page at all, so the
! page's JS never downloads or runs. Much more effective than hiding on a phone.
! A "blocked by uBO" page with a click-through appears instead. uBO has no
! password: anyone can disable it. See README.md in this folder for real safeguards.

! ── YouTube ─────────────────────────────────────
! Shorts: never load a /shorts/ URL
||youtube.com/shorts/$document
||m.youtube.com/shorts/$document
! Trending / Explore feeds
||youtube.com/feed/trending$document
||youtube.com/feed/explore$document
! Home page itself — uncomment to refuse "/" entirely. Use a bookmark to
! https://www.youtube.com/feed/subscriptions as your entry point instead.
! |https://www.youtube.com/^$document
! |https://m.youtube.com/^$document

! ── Instagram ───────────────────────────────────
||instagram.com/reels/$document
||instagram.com/reel/$document
||instagram.com/explore/$document

! ── TikTok: block the whole site ────────────────
||tiktok.com^$document
||tiktokv.com^
||tiktokcdn.com^

! ── Facebook Reels / Watch ──────────────────────
||facebook.com/reel/$document
||facebook.com/watch/$document

! ── Reddit r/popular, r/all ─────────────────────
||reddit.com/r/popular$document
||reddit.com/r/all$document

! ── X Explore / trends ──────────────────────────
||x.com/explore$document
||twitter.com/explore$document

! ── Autoplay ────────────────────────────────────
! Can't be done with filters: a preview stream and a tapped video come from the
! same host. Use instead:
!   YouTube → Settings → Playback → "Inline playback" off
!   Firefox → Settings → Site permissions → Autoplay → "Block audio and video"
!   uBO → Settings → "Block media elements larger than … KB" (stops big video
!   loads until you click them; set 50–100 KB on mobile data)
